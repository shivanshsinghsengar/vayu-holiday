Journey Overview

Experience the ethereal magic of Kashmir in supreme comfort. Handcrafted by Vayu Holidays, this journey balances peaceful moments on cedar-wood royal houseboats with the thrilling alpine grandeur of Gulmarg and the tranquil pine meadows of Pahalgam.

Day-wise Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival in Srinagar & Royal Dal Lake Shikara
VIP arrival at Srinagar Airport. Transfer to luxury heritage houseboat. Sunset Shikara ride across Dal Lake. Welcome Kahwa tea ceremony. Stay: Luxury Heritage Houseboat (Nigeen/Dal Lake)
Day 02
Srinagar to Gulmarg: Meadow of Flowers & Gondola
Scenic drive to Gulmarg (2,730m). Board Gulmarg Gondola Phase 1 & 2 — one of the highest in the world. Spectacular Nanga Parbat views. Stay: Boutique Alpine Resort, Gulmarg
Day 03
Gulmarg to Pahalgam via Saffron Fields
Drive through Pampore saffron fields and Awantipora ruins. Arrive Pahalgam beside the Lidder River. Stay: Riverside Luxury Resort, Pahalgam
Day 04
Pahalgam: Betaab Valley, Aru & Chandanwari
Full day in pristine Betaab Valley and Aru Valley surrounded by snow peaks. Evening stroll through Pahalgam bazaars. Stay: Riverside Luxury Resort, Pahalgam
Day 05
Pahalgam to Srinagar & Mughal Gardens
Private heritage tour of Shalimar Bagh, Nishat Bagh, Shankaracharya Temple with panoramic valley views. Stay: 5-Star Hotel / Luxury Resort, Srinagar
Day 06
Departure
Leisurely morning Kahwa breakfast. Chauffeur transfer to Srinagar Airport with cherished memories.
