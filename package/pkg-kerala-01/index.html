Immerse yourself in God's Own Country. Drift through the palm-fringed tranquility of Vembanad Lake on a private luxury houseboat and breathe the crisp mountain air in the misty rolling tea plantations of Munnar.

Day-wise Itinerary

Day 01
Cochin to Munnar: Into the Misty Hills
Arrive Cochin Airport. Scenic drive through Cheeyappara waterfalls into Munnar. Stay: Luxury Tea Resort, Munnar
Day 02
Munnar: Tea Museum, Eravikulam & Mattupetty
Eravikulam National Park (Nilgiri Tahr), Tata Tea Museum, Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point. Spice garden tour. Stay: Luxury Tea Resort, Munnar
Day 03
Munnar to Thekkady: Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary
Bamboo rafting on Periyar Lake. Spot elephants and birdlife. Evening Kathakali cultural show. Stay: Forest Canopy Retreat, Thekkady
Day 04
Thekkady to Alleppey: Private Luxury Houseboat
Board private luxury houseboat at noon. Cruise backwater canals. Freshly prepared onboard chef meals. Stay: Private Premium Houseboat, Alleppey
Day 05
Alleppey to Cochin Departure
Morning breakfast cruise. Transfer to Cochin Airport or railway station for departure.
