Day-wise Itinerary

Day 01
Arrival Colombo – Dambulla Caves
Drive to cultural triangle. Dambulla Cave Temple with ancient Buddhist murals. Stay: 4-Star Hotel, Dambulla/Sigiriya
Day 02
Sigiriya Rock Climb & Polonnaruwa
Sunrise climb of Sigiriya Lion Rock with frescoes. Afternoon UNESCO medieval city of Polonnaruwa. Stay: 4-Star Hotel, Dambulla/Sigiriya
Day 03
Dambulla to Kandy: Spice Garden & Temple of Tooth
Spice garden, Temple of Sacred Tooth Relic, Kandyan cultural dance. Stay: 4-Star Hotel, Kandy
Day 04
Kandy to Nuwara Eliya: Tea Country
Optional scenic train ride. Ceylon tea plantation & factory. Colonial hill station. Stay: 4-Star Hotel, Nuwara Eliya
Day 05
Bentota Beach & Madu River Safari
Transfer to coast. Mangrove boat safari, turtle hatchery, golden beach. Stay: 4-Star Beach Resort, Bentota
Day 06
Bentota to Colombo & Departure
Short Colombo city tour & shopping. Transfer to airport.
