Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are needed for a Schengen visa?
Valid passport (6+ months), accurate day-wise itinerary, confirmed hotel reservations, travel insurance (€30,000 minimum), 6-month bank statements, employment proof, and a detailed cover letter. Our visa desk provides a complete personalized checklist.
Which countries offer instant e-Visas?
UAE, Vietnam, Indonesia (Bali), Sri Lanka, Azerbaijan, and Malaysia offer fast-track e-Visas, which we process within 24 to 72 hours.
How long does Schengen visa processing take?
Typically 10–15 working days from biometric appointment. We recommend applying 6–8 weeks before travel. Peak season (June–September) can be longer.
