Our Story

Founded with the vision to deliver original, minimal luxury travel, Vayu Holidays combines the warmth of personal hospitality with the precision of an international travel atelier. Headquartered at Raksha Vihar, Vayu Residency, Airport Road in Bhopal, we serve discerning families, honeymoon couples, and corporate delegations across Madhya Pradesh and nationwide. We eliminate guesswork by delivering itemized proposals with zero hidden costs.
